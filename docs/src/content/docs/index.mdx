## What Grove Does

<CardGrid>
    <Card title="Spaces" icon="open-book">
        Grove Spaces are structured containers that hold the projects, tools, resources, and
        knowledge a community curates. Spaces are versioned, forkable, and easy to maintain.
    </Card>
    <Card title="Blueprints" icon="setting">
        Blueprints define the schema of a Space. They make contributions reviewable, keep quality
        consistent, and let contributors submit pull requests with confidence.
    </Card>
    <Card title="Open Source First" icon="rocket">
        Grove is MIT-licensed, framework-agnostic, and ships adapters for Astro, Next.js, and
        SvelteKit. The data lives in your repo, the tooling fits your existing CI.
    </Card>
    <Card title="Grow Over Time" icon="starlight">
        Grove treats knowledge like a long-lived artifact. Updates, refactors, and archival are
        first-class operations, not afterthoughts.
    </Card>
</CardGrid>
